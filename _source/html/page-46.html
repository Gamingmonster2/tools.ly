How This Tool Works

This calculator is different from “Ideal Weight” tools. It ignores standard height/weight charts and instead focuses on **your actual body composition** (your muscle vs. fat).

You must use a measuring tape for this to be accurate.

  1. First, we use your measurements (Height, Neck, Waist, and Hips for women) in the **U.S. Navy Body Fat Formula** to estimate your current body fat percentage.
  2. From that, we calculate your **Lean Body Mass (LBM)** — this is the weight of your muscles, bones, and organs. This is the “good” weight we want to protect.
  3. Finally, we calculate the goal: We show you how much fat you need to lose to reach a healthy and athletic body fat percentage (approx. 15% for men, 22% for women) **while keeping all your current muscle.**

Disclaimer: The tool is still under development, and we are working on improving it. The values provided are **estimates** based on statistical formulas and are for informational purposes only. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice. You should contact a nutritionist, doctor, or your trainer for personalized advice.

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure that uses your height and weight to work out if your weight is healthy. For most adults, an ideal BMI is in the 18.5 to 24.9 range.

BMI Categories:

  • Underweight: less than 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25 – 29.9
  • Obesity: 30 or greater

This calculator is intended for adults aged 20 and over. For children and teens, different charts are used.

Understanding Your Ideal Weight

This calculator provides an ideal weight range based on the **Healthy Body Mass Index (BMI)** guidelines. The World Health Organization (WHO) defines a healthy BMI range as being between **18.5 and 24.9**.

Your "ideal weight" is the weight range that allows you to fall within this healthy BMI category for your height. This is a more modern and widely accepted measure than older formulas that only use height and gender.

Remember, this is a healthy range, not a single "perfect" number. Factors like muscle mass, body frame, and overall health also play a significant role. Always consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.

What is Body Fat Percentage?

This tool estimates how much of your total body weight is made up of fat.

Why is it useful? The number on your scale doesn't tell the whole story. A high weight could be due to healthy muscle, while a "normal" weight might hide a high level of fat.

Knowing your body fat percentage gives you a much clearer picture of your health and fitness than BMI alone. This calculator uses the U.S. Navy method, which relies on simple tape measurements to estimate your body composition.
