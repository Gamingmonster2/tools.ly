About tools.ly

Welcome to tools.ly, your go-to destination for a curated collection of completely **free online tools** designed to make everyday tasks easier — from tracking weight loss to analysing smartwatch data, and much more.

Our Mission

At tools.ly, our mission is simple: we believe that access to quality digital tools should be open to everyone. That’s why we develop and share useful, web-based tools **at no cost**, with no hidden fees, no subscriptions, and no barriers. Our goal is to empower users to take control of their fitness, gadgets, productivity and personal goals with confidence and ease.

What You’ll Find Here

Every tool on tools.ly is:

  • Developed in-house by us and regularly updated for accuracy and performance.
  • Easy to use — just open the tool, input your data, and get meaningful results.
