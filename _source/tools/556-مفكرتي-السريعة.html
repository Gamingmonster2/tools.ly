About This Secure Quick Notepad

This is a simple yet effective online notepad designed for quickly jotting down notes and ideas. It offers a clean interface and essential features for note-taking.

Key Features:

  • Instant Saving: Your notes are saved automatically to your browser’s local storage as you type.
  • Local Storage: All your notes are stored directly on your device (in your browser). This ensures your privacy, as no data is ever sent to our servers.
  • Clear Text: Easily wipe the current text area to start fresh.
  • Delete Notes: Individual notes can be deleted from your saved list.
  • Daily Note Limit: To prevent misuse and ensure fair usage, there’s a limit of 5 new notes per day. This resets every 24 hours. You can still view, clear, and delete old notes freely.

Privacy Notice: We value your privacy. Your notes are stored exclusively in your web browser’s local storage and are never transmitted to our servers. If you clear your browser’s data or use a different browser/device, your notes will not be accessible.
